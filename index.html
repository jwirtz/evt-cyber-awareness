What future you will do next time

1

Hover before you click

This link pointed to github.io, not anything at evtcorp.com. Your mouse would have told you that for free.

2

Notice the vague ask

"For more information, click here" with no details is exactly how bad links get clicked. Ask yourself where it goes and why.

3

When in doubt, ask us

A 30 second message to the security team beats a 30 day incident. We never mind the question.

If this had been the real thing

Clicked something sketchy, entered a password somewhere odd, or approved an MFA prompt you didn't start? Tell the EVT security team right away. We can kill your sessions in minutes, but only if we know.
